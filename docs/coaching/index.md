## Why Gen Alpha Needs a Different Approach

Gen Alpha (born 2013-2025) is growing up in a world where AI is as fundamental as electricity. They won't be "using" AI—they'll be *thinking with it*. The question isn't whether to introduce AI, but how to guide its use for learning and growth.

## What We Offer

Our GenAI coaching program helps parents empower their children to:

- **Master study workflows**: Structure homework and project work with AI assistance
- **Develop critical thinking**: Learn to question, verify, and synthesize AI-generated content
- **Future-proof their skills**: Build capabilities that will thrive in an AI-augmented workforce
